# Oasis and dune places

Oasis Citadel replaces two small generic building groups with five separately arranged settlements: caravan courts, an awning market, spring-side homes, a western court and a sunset bazaar. Open ventilated wind towers, adobe roof terraces, arched doors, shaded verandas, market counters and courtyard basins have solid original geometry. Seven broad mixed palm gardens and a sandstone ridge frame the places, while open desert sections remain.

Dune Serpent alternates its long changing-radius bends with four groups of narrow sandstone blades and two travelers' rest stops. The geological meshes have different proportions and orientations and closed foundations embedded in actual terrain. The well court sits beside a mixed palm hollow. Existing fossil ribs remain, with no new repeated road gates.

Full building footprints are disjoint; palm canopies reserve both buildings and rocks. All 34 courses pass the authored composition check, with 27 physical modules at Oasis and 20 at Dune. Original terrain exports and gameplay compatibility are unchanged. Native views and actual course photographs are retained here; before/after images use the same eleven Far Chase locations per course. These local renders do not establish physical Android or human driving acceptance.

All twelve focused commands pass: authoring consistency, asset/gameplay hashes, frozen terrain, script compilation, authored placements, scenery/sightlines, small service scenes, biome surfaces, hero set pieces and shader warmup. Checks and logs are retained.
