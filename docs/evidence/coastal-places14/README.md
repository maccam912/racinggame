# Coastal places

Coral Causeway has nine solid limestone bluffs grouped into three headlands, four cottage and boat-club settlements, varied palms and pale sand. Monsoon Delta has three stilt villages with open boat sheds, fishing decks and deep riverbank forests under a gray sky. Mangrove Maze retains its enclosed canopy stretches and gains a fishing settlement and deeper woodland. Each has its own water colors. Road-facing fronts in these settlements and Marina now face the course; courtyard-facing buildings retain their intended orientation.

The original code-built models have pitched roofs, verandas, stairs, shutters, open stilts, boats on cradles and covered fishing decks. These are bank settlements on supported dry ground. Building bounds are disjoint, plant canopies reserve building footprints, and buried geological bases stay below actual exported terrain. No road, terrain export, collision or gameplay hash changed.

Eleven matching native Far Chase views per coastal course are retained in `before/` and `after/`, with their capture sources and logs. Four fresh actual course photographs also update the shipped 512×288 thumbnails. Fourteen focused source commands pass, including all 34 authored compositions, scenery and sightline contracts, watercraft, small services, biome surfaces, all 30 set pieces, shader warmup and the original ten-course variety tests.

These are local source checks and native macOS scenes, separate from physical Android, exported-build and human driving acceptance.

The isolated production-client comparison completed Coral, Mangrove and Monsoon with all eight cars in each race, at Medium, 1920×1080 and a 60 fps cap. Before/after recorded 15,241/15,243 racing frames: p95 17.956/17.592 ms, p99 18.816/18.512 ms, maximum 35.850/32.607 ms, and 2/0 frames above 33 ms. The retained reports omit only per-frame spatial arrays.
